FIRST AUTHOR · IEEE ACCESS, 2026

LiDAR localization in challenging spaces

Problem
Sparse scans and repetitive geometry make it difficult to align LiDAR measurements reliably.
Contribution
First-author research on spatial-context-aware scan registration using LiDAR alone, evaluated in CERN facilities.
Result
Lower registration error and improved map quality against the comparison methods in the paper’s four tested environments.

FIRST AUTHOR · 3D MAPPING

DynaHull

Problem
Moving people leave unwanted points in indoor maps.
Contribution
A density-based filtering method that separates dynamic points from static structure.
Result
Improved map similarity and filtering metrics against the evaluated methods in a dynamic indoor setting.

03

CO-AUTHOR · EVENT-BASED VISION

Drone trajectory forecasting

Problem
Fast-moving drones are difficult to track and predict.
Contribution
Co-authored a method combining propeller-speed cues from event data with Kalman filtering.
Result
Lower prediction errors than the evaluated baselines on FRED at 0.4 s and 0.8 s horizons.
Read paper ↗

CO-CREATOR · OPEN DATA

CERN LiDAR dataset

Problem
Mapping methods need data from difficult real environments.
Contribution
Co-created a collection of Unitree LiDAR scans and IMU recordings at CERN.
Result
Data from six environments, openly available for benchmarking under CC BY 4.0.

03 / PROFILE+

About

I’m a doctoral researcher at Aalto University’s Autonomy & Mobility Lab, working on localization and mapping for mobile robots. My focus is a practical one: helping robots navigate spaces where geometry is repetitive, sensor data is sparse, and the world keeps moving.

My background spans mechanical and aerospace engineering at Kyushu University, mechatronics at Aalto, and a seven-month research visit to CERN. Alongside research, I work on applied robotics through PJM Robotics and share what I learn through hands-on teaching.
